# Utah Redistricting Standards

*Utah Code § 20A-19-103(2)(b–g), (3); Utah Code § 20A-20-302(4). Legislature process; advisory commission plan is non-binding.*

## Population

- Achieve equal population among districts to the extent required
- **Congressional districts:** total population deviation must not exceed **1%**

## District shape

- Districts must be **contiguous** and allow ease of transportation throughout the district
- Districts must be **geographically compact**

## Communities & boundaries

- Preserve **traditional neighborhoods and local communities of interest**
- **Minimize division of municipalities and counties** across multiple districts
  - First priority: minimize municipality splits
  - Second priority: minimize county splits
- Follow **natural and geographic features, boundaries, and barriers**
- Preserve **cores of prior districts**
- Maximize **boundary agreement** among different types of districts
